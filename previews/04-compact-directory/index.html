Sheaf Theory for Non-Exact Lagrangians

Tatsuki Kuwagaki·Kyoto University

Sheaf-theoretic methods in symplectic geometry have grown rapidly since Nadler–Zaslow and Tamarkin. I will present a modification of Tamarkin’s approach for non-exact Lagrangians and a comparison theorem with Fukaya-type categories, with remarks on homological mirror symmetry.

Stability and expansion

Markus Reineke·Ruhr University Bochum

We propose expansion in abelian categories as a quantitative refinement of slope stability, related to dimension expanders. Main result: uniform expansion for representations of wild quivers via spectral properties of Cartan matrices.

The Loop-Nilpotent cohomological Hall Algebra and Coulomb Branches

Shivang Jindal·EPF Lausanne

Kontsevich–Soibelman defined the CoHA as a model for the algebra of BPS states. I will introduce the loop-nilpotent CoHA as a subalgebra of a shuffle algebra cut out by wheel conditions, with a surjection onto half of a quantized Coulomb-branch algebra. Joint with Andrei Neguț.

From locality to homotopy theory: higher symmetries and anomalies

Anton Kapustin·Caltech and IHES

Symmetries of extended quantum systems form a higher group whose Postnikov data encode ’t Hooft anomalies. I will build these homotopy-theoretic invariants from algebras of local observables via crossed precosheaves of groups, with $U(1)$ gauge-theory examples.

Maxim Kontsevich·IHES

Orientifold lines from CoHA modules

Sam DeHority·Yale University

A category of CoHA modules with vertex coaction models line operators in a $4d$ $\mathcal{N}=2$ theory; folding gives modules for orientifold lines, with applications to representations of quantized Coulomb-branch algebras.
